A top-down stealth heist game, built for the [best heist game bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559) on make.fun.

You are the thief. Cross three rooms — a museum gallery, a bank vault, a
penthouse — grab the loot and reach the getaway exit without letting the
alarm hit 100%.

- **Guards** patrol with red vision cones; **cameras** sweep amber cones.
  Being seen fills the alarm meter; breaking line of sight cools it down.
- **Tools:** smoke bombs (×2 per room, cones cannot see through smoke),
  a camera jammer (×1), and thrown coins to lure a guard away.
- **Fair catches:** the alarm gives you time to vanish, and a chasing
  guard can be outrun — but touch one while it chases and the job is blown.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html`.
WASD/arrows to move, click to throw a coin, `1` for smoke, `2` to jam,
`R` to retry a room.
